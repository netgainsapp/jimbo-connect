## 1. Launch blog post (~500 words)

**Title:** Introducing Intro Connect: keep the connections you make at events

**Body:**

You go to an event. You meet a handful of genuinely interesting people. You swap a
name, maybe a LinkedIn, promise to "follow up" — and two weeks later you're staring at
a business card trying to remember which conversation it belonged to.

The connections were real. The follow-through is where it falls apart.

We built Intro Connect to fix exactly that. It's a simple idea: give every event a
home where the people who were actually in the room can find each other again, remember
who they talked to, and keep the conversation going — long after the name tags come off.

**How it works**

- **Hosts create an event** and get a join code (or a shareable link). No app store,
  no friction — attendees join in seconds.
- **Attendees build a lightweight profile** — who they are, what they're working on,
  what they're looking for. Enough to be findable and worth reaching out to.
- **Meet someone worth remembering? Save them.** Add a note while it's fresh ("the
  founder building the logistics thing — intro to Sam").
- **Message directly, right in the app.** No hunting for emails, no cold-LinkedIn
  awkwardness. You were in the same room; the context is already there.

That's the whole loop: join, meet, save, stay in touch. It works for conferences,
meetups, workshops, dinners, retreats — anywhere the value is the people.

**Why we made it free to start**

Hosting your first event on Intro Connect is free. We'd rather you feel the value —
the post-event Slack-that-never-happened, replaced by connections people actually keep
— than talk you into it. If it works for your community, growing with you is easy.

**Try it**

If you host events, spin one up in a couple of minutes: **[app.intro-connect.com](https://app.intro-connect.com)**.
If you're an attendee, ask your next host if they're on Intro Connect — or send them our way.

Better events, stronger connections. That's the whole point.
