## 1 — Alex Zarookian, Denver Metro Chamber of Commerce

**To:** alex.zarookian@denverchamber.org
**Subject:** what members remember after Speed Networking

Hi Alex,

Your members come to Speed Networking to meet people. Then the event ends, the name tags go in the bin, and most of those conversations never turn into anything. When renewal time comes, "I met someone interesting once" is a hard memory to bill against.

I built Intro Connect to close that gap. It turns each of your events into a private directory of everyone who came. Members browse who they met, save contacts, message each other, and the value of showing up becomes something they can see all year.

Guests never pay and never install anything. They join in the browser in about a minute.

Your first event is free for up to 50 attendees. Worth a look before your next session? https://intro-connect.com

Scott Weiss
Founder, Intro Connect
