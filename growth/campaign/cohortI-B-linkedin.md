# Cohort I-B: LinkedIn target list (compiled, 2026-09-05)

The LinkedIn lane of the channel test, expanded by the 4-agent harvest. Named
programming/events and membership owners (plus a president where useful) at
professional association chapters. Scott sends connection notes from his OWN
profile. Every LinkedIn URL below was pulled verbatim from the chapter's board
page (never constructed); name-only rows are findable by name + chapter.

**Why LinkedIn for this lane:** AMA/PRSA/IABC/NAWBO chapters publish boards as
LinkedIn profiles, not emails. This is the rich vein. NOTE LinkedIn limits
connection requests to roughly 15-20/day before flagging, so this lane paces
slower than email regardless of supply.

**Do NOT double-contact:** AMA Charlotte (Aleka Thrash) is in the EMAIL cohort
(cohortI-A-email.md), so it is omitted here. One channel per person.
