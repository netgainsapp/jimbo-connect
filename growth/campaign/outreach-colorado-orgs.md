## Reply playbook

- **"How much?"** Free for the first event, 50 attendees. Then $39 a month or
  $390 a year for 3 events at 250 people, $99 a month or $990 a year for
  unlimited events at 2,000. Members and guests never pay anything.
- **"Is our member data safe?"** Guests see each other's profiles, never each
  other's email addresses. Messaging only works between people who attended
  together. You can export nothing you did not already have: it is your guest
  list.
- **"Can it match our branding?"** Your logo and color on event pages and
  guest emails on the Pro plan.
- **"We use [Eventbrite or similar]."** Keep it. They sell tickets before the
  event; we keep the room connected after it. The guest list imports straight
  across.
- **Wants a demo:** send the walkthrough link, then offer the founding host
  arrangement (founding-host-offer.md) if they run recurring events.
