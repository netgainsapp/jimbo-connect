# Blog topic calendar for the automated engine

Twelve topics for the existing blog pipeline, ordered. Each maps to a search
intent an event host actually types, and each ends with the same soft close:
the free plan or the free agenda builder, whichever the topic earns.

Two per month is the pace; the engine can run faster but Google does not care
and quality drops. Topics 1 and 5 target the agenda builder funnel where we
already have the tool nobody else gives away free.

1. **How to follow up after a networking event (without being weird)**
   Intent: attendee AND host searches. The pain in our own words; the
   directory as the fix for the whole room at once.
2. **Event agenda template: build one in 10 minutes and export to Word**
   Intent: "event agenda template word". The agenda builder IS the answer;
   this should become the highest converting post on the site.
3. **How chambers of commerce prove membership value with events**
   Intent: track A reader. Renewal math, visible member benefit, the
   directory as receipts for "you will meet people here."
4. **How to run a founder dinner people talk about for years**
   Intent: track B reader. Curation, table size, the follow through gap, and
   staying at the center of the network you assembled.
5. **Free alternatives to expensive event apps (honest comparison)**
   Intent: "event app free". Honest table: what paid event apps do that we do
   not (ticketing, check-in hardware), what we do that they do not (the after,
   free forever for guests).
6. **The 48 hours after your event matter more than the event**
   Intent: host improvement searches. The decay curve of unforced follow up;
   what to send, when, and what a directory changes.
7. **How to import a guest list from Eventbrite or a spreadsheet**
   Intent: exact product task. Doubles as documentation; quoted CSV names and
   messy phone numbers welcome, because ours handles them.
8. **Event survey questions: why three is the right number**
   Intent: "post event survey questions". Our deliberately narrow survey as a
   philosophy: three questions, 1 to 5, totals not names, and why response
   rates follow.
9. **Networking event ideas that actually connect people**
   Intent: high volume host search. Formats that force mixing; ends with
   keeping the mixing alive afterward.
10. **How to get sponsors for a small event (and what to give them)**
    Intent: "get sponsors small event". Sponsor tiles from a URL as the
    lightest sponsor deliverable that exists.
11. **Should your event have an app? Usually no. Here is the exception.**
    Intent: contrarian, shareable. Install friction kills adoption; the
    browser directory as the exception that guests actually use.
12. **What to write in an event invitation email (templates included)**
    Intent: "event invitation email template". Real copy blocks readers can
    steal, and the note that hosts on Intro Connect can make these their
    default in the product.

House rules for the engine, regardless of topic: no fabricated statistics, no
invented customer stories, claims about the product checked against it, and
the soft close never promises anything the pricing page does not.
