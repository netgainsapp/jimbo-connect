# Demo video script: 90 seconds

Brand voiceover, screen recording only, no faces per the brand only decision.
One video, three uses: the outreach walkthrough link, the site, and pinned on
both social profiles. Record at 1080p over the real product with seeded demo
data (use plausible fake people, never real guests).

Voice: plain and warm, medium pace, no music bed louder than the voice.
Runtime target 85 to 95 seconds.

---

**[0:00 to 0:08] The problem. Screen: a photo of a busy mixer, then a messy
spreadsheet of names.**

VO: You got the right people into a room. They met, it worked, and by Monday
it is a spreadsheet nobody opens.

**[0:08 to 0:20] The turn. Screen: the event page loading, the attendee grid
filling in with photos.**

VO: This is the same event on Intro Connect. Everyone who came, with a photo,
what they do, and what they are looking for. Private to the people who were
in the room, and it stays live long after the night.

**[0:20 to 0:35] The guest. Screen: search "robotics", open a profile, tap
save, type a private note, send a short message.**

VO: Your guests search the room, save the people they mean to follow up with,
keep a private note, and message directly. Nobody's email address gets handed
around, and there is nothing to install. It runs in the browser.

**[0:35 to 0:50] The host. Screen: Import guests modal, paste a list, the
preview counts rows, import, invitation email opens on a phone.**

VO: For you, setup is one paste. Bring the guest list from a spreadsheet or
Eventbrite, and every guest gets an invitation with their own sign in, in
your words if you want, because the email templates are yours to edit.

**[0:50 to 1:05] After the night. Screen: post an announcement, the survey
totals with their little bars, the agenda on the page.**

VO: Afterward, the room keeps working. Post an announcement everyone sees.
Ask three questions, answered one to five, and see totals, never names. The
agenda, the calendar file, the sponsors: all on one page.

**[1:05 to 1:20] The promise. Screen: pricing page, slow scroll.**

VO: Guests never pay. Ever. Hosts start free with a full event for fifty
people, and pay only when the room outgrows it.

**[1:20 to 1:30] Close. Screen: the homepage, URL large.**

VO: Your event should outlive the night. Intro Connect. First event free, at
intro connect dot com.

---

Production notes:
- Seed the demo event the morning of recording; check no real user data is
  visible anywhere, including browser tabs and the account menu.
- Record the import with the sample CSV from the QA package media folder,
  which includes a quoted "Doe, Jane" so the preview looks real.
- Captions burned in; most feeds play muted.
- Export one 16:9 master, one 1:1 cut of 0:08 to 0:35 for feed posts.
