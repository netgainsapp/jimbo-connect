# Intro Connect positioning (campaign source of truth)

Every other file in this folder derives from this one. If copy anywhere
contradicts this document, this document wins. Written 2026-07-31 under three
constraints from the founder: brand voice only, zero ad budget, outreach first.

## The one line

**Your event should outlive the night.**

Longer form: Intro Connect turns any event into a private directory of everyone
who came, so the connections made in the room survive the week after it.

## Who we are selling to (two tracks)

### Track A: Colorado business organizations
Chambers, young professionals groups, business associations, coworking
communities, economic development orgs. The person is a membership or events
director. Their event is a member benefit, and their renewal pitch is "you will
meet people here."

Their pain, in their words: "People come to the mixer, have a great
conversation, and never find each other again. Then they wonder what
membership is for."

What we sell them: proof that membership works. The directory makes the
networking value of every event visible and permanent, which is a renewal
argument, not just a nice evening.

### Track B: Founder and CEO dinner hosts (national)
Curated dinners, masterminds, invite-only clubs. The person is the curator.
The guest list IS their product; their reputation is the room they assemble.

Their pain: they spend weeks curating twelve perfect guests, the dinner is
electric, and by Monday it is twelve people with vague memories and no way to
reach each other. The host does follow-up intros by hand or not at all.

What we sell them: the network they already built, made real. They stay at the
center of it, every dinner compounds the last one, and intros stop depending
on their inbox.

not on this list without checking the product first; this company cuts untrue
copy on sight.

2. Free plan is the self serve close: run one real event, feel the value.
3. The 50 attendee cap and 1 event cap are the upgrade moments. In-app

## Objections and answers

**"We already share a spreadsheet or WhatsApp group."**
A spreadsheet is a list; this is a room. Photos so people remember each other,
search, direct messages, and nobody's email address gets passed around. And it
is the host's, not scattered across everyone's phones.

**"Our attendees will not join another app."**
There is no app to install. Guests tap a link or enter a code in the browser,
add a photo, done. Joining takes about a minute at the event itself.

**"Privacy concerns."**
Messaging only works between people who shared a room. Email addresses are
never shown to other guests. The cross event directory is opt in per event and
off by default. Attendees never pay and never see ads.

**"What does it cost my guests?"**
Nothing, ever.

**"We are too small to pay for this."**
The free plan is a full event for up to 50 people. Pay only when you outgrow it.

## Voice rules (apply to every word in this folder)

- Plain, warm, specific. Short sentences. No jargon.
- **No hyphens or em dashes in outward copy unless grammatically standard.**
- No emoji.
- Brand signature: "The Intro Connect team". No personal names in outbound.
- Claims must be true today. When in doubt, check the product, then write.
- Numbers exact: $39, $99, $390, $990, 50, 250, 2,000.

## Sending rules (operational, non negotiable)

- Outbound never sends from hello@intro-connect.com. That is the transactional
  sender for password resets and invitations; one spam complaint wave there
  breaks the product. Outbound goes through signal-scout as an Intro Connect
  tenant on its own sending identity.
- Three touches maximum, stop on any reply, one thread, real reply-to,
  unsubscribe and physical address in the footer (signal-scout handles it).
- Review every list by hand before it sends. Auto filtered lists are seeds,
  not send lists.
