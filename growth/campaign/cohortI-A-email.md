# Cohort I-A: email cohort (compiled from the 4-agent harvest, 2026-09-05)

Built for the 50/day scale-up. Four parallel harvest agents swept AAF ad clubs,
AMA chapters, PRSA/IABC chapters, and commercial-real-estate associations
(CREW/ULI/NAIOP/CoreNet). Every address below was verified as literally present
on the cited page, name-consistency checked, personal Gmail/employer addresses
rejected, deduped against the tracker. Nothing constructed.

Two tiers. TIER 1 is the strict named-quality cohort (Scott's A2 bar): each
address is either name-consistent to a specific person or a role alias printed
directly beside a named officer, so it reaches a real decision maker. TIER 2 is
the published-role-inbox pool (generic org inboxes, no name attached) for the
50/day VOLUME push, kept separate so sending it is a deliberate choice.
