Your event should outlive the night.

Intro Connect turns any event into a private directory of everyone who came, so the connections made in the room survive the week after it.

The problem

You get the right people into a room and they have real conversations. Then everyone goes home, and by Monday it is business cards in a drawer and first names without last names. The follow up that should happen mostly does not.

How it works
1

Create the event. You get a join code and a link. About five minutes.

2

Guests join in the browser at the event, or arrive imported from your spreadsheet, Eventbrite, or Meetup. Nothing to install.

3

The directory stays live. People find each other, message directly, and the room keeps talking.
