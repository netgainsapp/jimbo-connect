# Brand social calendar: first two weeks

Channels: LinkedIn company page and X, brand voice only. Same idea both
places, LinkedIn gets the longer cut, X gets the sharp one. Cadence: three
posts a week; more than that from a brand account with no following reads as
shouting into a room. Every post is true today; no engagement bait.

Practical note: a brand page with zero followers reaches almost nobody on its
own. These posts exist so the page looks alive when outreach recipients check
us out, which they will. Treat the page as a landing surface, not a channel.

---

## Week 1

**Post 1 (Mon): the flag in the ground**
LinkedIn: The best hour of any networking event is the week after it, and
almost nobody gets that hour. People meet, mean to follow up, and lose each
other by Monday. We built Intro Connect so any event becomes a private
directory of everyone who came: photos, roles, what people are looking for,
direct messages. Guests never pay and never install anything. The first event
is free. intro-connect.com
X: Business cards are where follow ups go to die. Intro Connect turns your
guest list into a private directory your attendees actually use. First event
free. Guests never pay. intro-connect.com

LinkedIn: If you run a monthly mixer, the same thing happens twelve times a
year: good conversations, zero follow through. A private attendee directory

they met. Import your guest list from a spreadsheet or Eventbrite in one
paste. intro-connect.com
X: Run a monthly event? The follow up gap costs you twelve times a year.
Close it once. intro-connect.com

## Week 2

**Post 4 (Mon): privacy as the feature**
LinkedIn: Networking tools usually leak. Ours is built the other way: guests
see each other's profiles, never each other's email addresses. Messages only
work between people who shared a room. The cross event directory is opt in
and off by default. Trust is why people fill in a real profile at all.
X: Your guests see profiles, never email addresses. Messaging only between
people who shared a room. Privacy is the feature. intro-connect.com
