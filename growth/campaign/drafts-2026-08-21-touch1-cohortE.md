## What this pass says about the sourcing constraint

Eleven young professionals and chamber pages plus six staff directories were
fetched and parsed. Six produced a named person with a published direct
address. That is roughly the same one-in-three hit rate the morning run got from
a much larger crawl, and it confirms the diagnosis rather than fixing it:
chambers increasingly route to forms, so the address, not the organisation, is
the binding constraint.

Young professionals pages are the better vein. They name a human contact far
more often than a chamber staff directory does, because the group is trying to
recruit members, and their cadence is higher.
