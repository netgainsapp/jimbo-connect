assembled the room on purpose and their reputation is the guest list. The
angle is: the network you curate is the product, and right now it evaporates
between dinners.

## Reply playbook

- **"My list is confidential."** Good. The directory is private to the people
  who were in the room, invisible to everyone else, and guests see profiles,
  never email addresses. The cross event directory is opt in and off by
  default; nobody leaks anywhere by default.
- **"My guests are senior; they will not fill out profiles."** Import does it
  for them: name, role, company from your spreadsheet. A guest who adds
  nothing still appears; a photo takes one tap at the table.
- **"How is this different from a WhatsApp group?"** A group chat is noise
  with no memory. This is a quiet, searchable room: no notifications begging
  for attention, and the person you met in March is still findable in
  October.
- **"Price?"** First event free, 50 seats, more than any dinner needs. Hosts
  running a series take Starter at $39 a month or $390 a year. Guests never
  pay.
- **Warm replies:** offer the founding host arrangement, then ask which
  dinner is next and set the event up for them from their guest list.
