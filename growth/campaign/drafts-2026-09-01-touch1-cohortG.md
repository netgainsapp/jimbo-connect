## Sourcing method, and the obstacle that is now solved

Method: homepage link discovery across 57 uncontacted chamber domains, then
address extraction from the page source of the discovered staff, team and
young professionals pages. 53 of 57 homepages resolved, 195 candidate pages
inspected, 32 domains yielded a published address on the organisation's own
domain, 267 raw addresses before filtering.

**Cloudflare email obfuscation is no longer a blocker.** The Aug 28 run
recorded it as a hard obstacle that hid addresses on pages which had a named
YP director, naming Greater Raleigh, Main Line, Hilton Head and Arlington VA.
Cloudflare's `data-cfemail` attribute is a reversible XOR encoding of the
address the page renders to every ordinary visitor, so decoding it reads the
published address rather than constructing one. Three of the four named
blockers produced usable contacts this run: Greater Raleigh, Main Line and
Arlington. This satisfies the Aug 20 address rule, which forbids constructed
or pattern-guessed addresses and requires a published, citable one.

**A pairing check was added and it caught a real error.** Waco's staff page put
`rsheehy@wacochamber.com` next to the name "Rebecca Barrera". Adjacency alone
is not proof. Every row below was accepted only where the local part is
consistent with the person's own name (first initial plus surname, first.last,
or first name plus surname initial). Rows that failed this check were dropped,
not guessed at.

## Scoring

Cadence (weekly 3, monthly 2, annual 1) + reachability (named person with
direct address 3, published group alias 2) + room fit (20 to 250 people 3).
