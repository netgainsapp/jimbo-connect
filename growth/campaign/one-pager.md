## The room empties. The connections should not.

**Intro Connect is an online platform that connects guests after the event
ends.** Your guests reach the people they meant to follow up with. Free to
run your next event. Attendees never pay.

### The problem

You get the right people into a room. They have real conversations. Then
everyone goes home, and by Monday it is business cards in a drawer and first
names without last names. The follow up that should happen mostly does not,
and the value you created quietly expires.

### What your guests get

- A private page of everyone who attended: photo, role, company, what they
  are looking for. Searchable, works on any phone, nothing to install.
- Direct messages to the people they met. No email addresses handed around.
- Saved contacts with private notes only they can see.
- The agenda, announcements from you, and add to calendar.
- **Free for guests, forever. They never pay and never see ads.**

### What you get

- Guest list import in one paste, from a spreadsheet or an Eventbrite export.
- Invitations sent automatically, in your words if you want: the email
  templates are editable per host.
- Announcements to the whole room, on the page and from your own email.
- Sponsor tiles from a pasted URL.
- The network you built, still yours next month.

### How it works

1. Create the event. You get a join code and a link. About five minutes.
2. Quickly import your list from a spreadsheet or an Eventbrite export.
3. After the night, the directory stays live and the room keeps talking.
