## Why 15 and not 25

Two sourcing methods were run.

1. Search led discovery into page source extraction: high yield. Roughly 30
   organisation pages inspected, 15 usable contacts.
2. Direct path probing, guessing `/young-professionals/`, `/ypn/`, `/yp/` and
   `/young-professionals-network/` across 42 chamber domains that had not been
   contacted: **168 pages probed, 2 usable contacts** (Wilmington NC and FMWF).
   That is a 1.2 percent hit rate and it confirms the 2026-08-21 finding.

The binding constraint remains the published address, not the supply of
organisations. Chambers increasingly route to a contact form, and a growing
share of those that do publish addresses hide them behind Cloudflare email
obfuscation, which defeats source extraction entirely. Cloudflare obfuscation
was present on 5 of the roughly 30 pages inspected by hand, including four that
otherwise had a named YP director and would have scored 8.

Recommendation, since this is now the third consecutive run to fall short of
25: the daily target of 25 is not reachable by public page extraction alone.
Either lower the daily target to a number the method can actually sustain,
around 10 to 15, or add a sourcing channel that does not depend on
organisations publishing addresses.
